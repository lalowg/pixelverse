02Pipeline

How a world gets made

Six stages, always in this order. The island on each card is the same one, built up a layer at a time.

Build1/6

  1. Stage 01 · Seed

    Every world starts as one sentence

    The Drift began as islands that wander, and the people who keep them from drifting apart. If a world can't fit in a sentence, it isn't ready for a level editor.

  2. Stage 02 · Landform

    Then we give it ground

    Terrain is blocked out in grey boxes and walked at player height until the distances feel right: far enough to wonder about, close enough to reach.

  3. Stage 03 · Ecology

    Life before story

    Plants, weather and creatures follow simple rules. Hushwood's fireflies started life as a pathfinding test and ended up as the forest's memory.

  4. Stage 04 · Settlement

    People need reasons to live here

    Every building answers three questions: who built it, what they fear, and what they would never throw away. Gearwind's mills exist because the island needs winding.

  5. Stage 05 · Lore

    Write the history nobody reads

    We write far more history than ever reaches the screen. Players rarely read it, but they feel it in where a ruin sits and what a road is called.

  6. Stage 06 · Play

    Only then, the game

    Once a world holds together, the mechanics almost suggest themselves. In The Drift you don't level up. The island does.

Fictional studio diary

  1. Q1Make an island floatComplete

    A cone of rock, a slab of grass and a camera that could orbit it. We spent a full week just looking at it from different angles before adding anything else.

    • Island hangs in the sky without looking stuck there
    • Camera can circle it in one gesture
    • Underside worth looking at
  2. Q2Teach the wind to carry soundComplete

    Gearwind's sails now set the tempo of the soundtrack. Stand downwind of the village and the music gets a little louder.

    • Wind direction drives the mills
    • Mills drive the music
    • Nobody can tell where the music ends and the wind begins
  3. Q3Populate HushwoodIn progress

    Fourteen creature sketches, three survivors so far. The fox stays. The fox was never in danger.

    • Fireflies remember conversations
    • Creatures react to the forest's hum
    • Night shift for the owls
  4. Q4Wake the Cinder CrownIn progress

    A day and night cycle that changes what the island can reach. When the Crown sleeps, the Drift sinks and lower islands come into view.

    • Dusk, night and dawn lighting
    • Island altitude follows the Crown's breathing
    • Something worth finding down there
  5. Q5Open the Quiet RingLocked

    Requires: a reason to leave. We're still writing it, and we won't open the door until the answer is good enough.

  6. Q6Invite the first playersUp next

    Worlds only come alive when someone else walks around in them. This is where you come in.
